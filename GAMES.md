# Richard Moore's games

Keep this up to date whenever Richard mentions a change. Starters copy the list of other games from here, and new names are checked against it.

Every game has its own repository and its own Foundry VTT system, like the one each new starter sets up.

| Game | Status | Repository | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Heisty Spideys* | Rulebook v4.8 with a fully automated Foundry VTT system (1.8.0); in group testing, then launch preparation | SpideyHeist | Spiders pulling heists in a house. Simulated, playtested and fixed through v4.8. The source of the toolchain in `templates/project/` |
| *Don't Get Forked* | Rulebook v0.3 (playtest copy); v0.2 was reviewed and played by Richard's group | DontGetForked | Monsters from a castle on their yearly raid on the town; getting "forked" |
| *Nine Lives Gang* | Rulebook v0.8, reviewed | KittenJob | Cats robbing houses; push-your-luck |
| *Cat Burglars* | Starter delivered; status not recorded | CBZ | Richard to confirm status |
| *Nightwake* (was *Elemental Fighters*) | Rulebook v0.1 | EF | Heroes of the five Chinese elements, each with a perk, a fighting style and an Awakening, fighting the Unmade (first pitched as "The Darkness"); one night, one shot |
| *Second Nature* (was the Morph game) | Rulebook v0.1 (playtest draft) | Animahs | Kids who each shift into one animal, with a human perk and an animal perk; inspired by *Animorphs*, so the name and terms must stay clear of it |
| *Don't Get Gobble Gobbled* | Starter delivered 2026-10-26 | GobblinGlobbin | Turkeys hiding and escaping on dinner night. The finished starter documents are in `examples/` |
