## Project-specific: Don't Get Gobble Gobbled
21. **Turkeys are real turkeys.** Two legs, each foot with three toes forward and one back. Include the snood (the flap over the beak), the wattle and the caruncles. Toms have a beard on the chest and spurs, and fan a tail of many feathers. Count the legs and toes on every turkey.
22. **Each player turkey is recognisable at a glance and stays the same everywhere.** Distinct real heritage colourings help: Bronze, Bourbon Red, Narragansett, Royal Palm, White. Keep a reference sheet per turkey, including left and right sides.
23. **Hiding spots are physically possible.** A turkey is big: about 1 m long for a tom. If one hides behind or inside something, it must really fit, and the hiding place must stand on something.
24. **Comic, not gruesome.** Ovens, roasting tins and carving knives stay cartoon threats. No raw-meat or butchery imagery.
25. **Holiday imagery without stereotypes.** No "Pilgrims and Indians" costumes or caricatures, and no Native American imagery used as decoration. A modern family dinner works anywhere a turkey is roasted (US or Canadian Thanksgiving, Christmas).
